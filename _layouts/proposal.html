About Golden

We're floral designers dedicated to creating artful arrangements in line with nature's cadence. By weaving local and seasonal flowers into our work, we celebrate each couple in a way that carries the unique beauty of every passing season.

Our Floral Philosophy

Three commitments behind every wedding

01

As designers

We prioritize style, color theory, and the details that make an arrangement feel considered rather than assembled.

02

As growers

We prioritize regenerative farming — much of what we design with is grown by our own hands, foam-free and in season.

03

As a vendor

We prioritize excellence and professionalism — clear communication, reliable timelines, and a seamless wedding day.

Reception Flowers

Low & lush centerpiece$300 / table

Lush but low to keep sightlines open, with height for depth. Best for round tables.

Bud vases$25–35 each

Lining the table, spaced 6–12 inches apart. Best for long, rectangular tables.

Full-service weddings over $8,000 include delivery, setup, repurposing of florals after the ceremony, and end-of-night strike — handled entirely by our team.

For weddings under $8,000, these are itemized: delivery $200, repurposing $200, strike $600.

Sales tax is added to the total cost of goods. Every figure here is a starting guide, refined in your proposal.
