{% comment %} WHY GOLDEN (pillars, placed just before the investment) {% endcomment %}
Why Golden

What your investment is built on

Led by Brittany

Our founder leads your design from the first consultation through setup and strike, alongside some of the region's best florists.

Farm-grown

We grow much of your palette on our own California farm and source the rest from growers we trust, never flown in from across the world.

Foam-free, by hand

Every arrangement is built by hand without floral foam. Better for your flowers, and for the landscape they came from.

Artist & agronomist

Brittany is an artist and an agronomist. Soil science shapes what we plant and source, and floral artistry shapes everything you see.
