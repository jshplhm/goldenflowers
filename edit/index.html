Click any text to edit it, then press Save. Click any photo to swap it. On a wedding page, Photos manages the whole gallery. Changes go live in about two minutes.

Photos

Add or remove photos, pick the big opening photo, or drag photos (or use the ‹ › buttons) into the order you want. Adds, removals and the opening photo save immediately; after dragging, press Save new order. Everything reaches the live site in about two minutes.

Swap this photo

Choose a new photo to appear in its place. The words and layout stay exactly the same.
