<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content><p style="white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Wedding flower budgets are one of the most Googled, and most misunderstood, parts of wedding planning. Here's an honest breakdown of what florals cost in the Lake Tahoe market, and the factors that move that number up or down.</p><p class="body-text" style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Let's start with the question everyone is actually asking: how much should I budget for wedding flowers? The honest answer is that it depends, but that answer is only useful if you understand what it depends on.</p><p class="body-text" style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">In the Lake Tahoe region, couples typically invest anywhere from $3,000 for a minimalist elopement to $15,000 or more for a fully florally-designed celebration. Most mid-size weddings (75–150 guests) land somewhere between $5,000 and $10,000. Here's what shapes that number.</p><h3 style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true"><strong>What you're actually paying for</strong></h3><p class="body-text" style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Wedding florals aren't just flowers, they're labor-intensive, time-sensitive, and deeply custom. Your florist's quote covers flower sourcing and procurement, design and arrangement time, delivery, on-site installation, and breakdown at the end of the night. For a full wedding, that can represent 20–40 hours of work behind the scenes before anyone walks down the aisle.</p><h3 style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true"><strong>Ballpark figures by floral element</strong></h3><p style="white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true"><strong>WHAT'S INCLUDEDTYPICAL RANGE </strong></p><p style="white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Bridal bouquet. Full, lush, hand-tied or structured $250 – $500+ </p><p style="white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Bridesmaid bouquets. smaller than bridal $95 – $175 each</p><p style="white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Boutonnieres. Per piece $50 – $65 each</p><p style="white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Ceremony arch / installation. Full floral or greenery structure $1,200 – $3,500+</p><p style="white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Centerpieces. Per table, low or elevated $150 – $500+ each. Bud vases $35 each</p><p style="white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Sweetheart / head table Garland, runners, or full installation $300 – $1,200+</p><p style="white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Cocktail hour florals. Bud vases, small arrangements $300 – $800</p><p style="white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Delivery, setup &amp; breakdownFull-service labor $400 – $900+</p><h3 style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true"><strong>What drives the cost up</strong></h3><p class="body-text" style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Guest count is the most obvious lever, more tables mean more centerpieces. But these factors move the number just as much:</p><p class="body-text" style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Flower variety and rarity. Garden roses, peonies, and ranunculus cost significantly more than carnations or standard spray roses. If your vision includes specific premium blooms, that's reflected in the quote. Seasonality also plays a role, flowers out of season are harder to source and more expensive.</p><p class="body-text" style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Complexity of design. A lush, layered ceremony arch with trailing greenery and three flower varieties takes far longer to build than a simple eucalyptus swag. Labor is a real cost in floral design.</p><p class="body-text" style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Venue access and logistics. Some Tahoe venues require specific installation windows, have restrictions on certain materials, or are located in areas that add significant travel and logistics time. These factors affect your quote.</p><h3 style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true"><strong>What drives the cost down</strong></h3><p class="body-text" style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Flexibility on specific stems is one of the best ways to stay on budget without sacrificing beauty. A skilled florist can build your color palet</p></div>
