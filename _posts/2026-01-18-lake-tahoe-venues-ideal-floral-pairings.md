<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>Lake Tahoe Wedding Venues and Perfect Floral Pairings </strong></p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Choosing a wedding venue is one of the most important decisions you'll make, as far as wedding planning goes, and Lake Tahoe offers some of the most breathtaking locations in the world. From elegant lakeside estates to elevated mountain lodges and luxe alpine retreats, each venue has its own unique character, architecture, and natural surroundings that should guide your floral design choices.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Choosing the right florals for your Lake Tahoe wedding venue is about creating harmony between your flowers, the venue's character, and the area’s stunning natural surroundings. Each venue has its own personality, from Edgewood's refined elegance to Olympic Valley Stables' rustic charm, from The Ritz-Carlton's mountain luxury to The Chateau's European romance.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The most successful Lake Tahoe wedding florals honor the venue's style while letting the natural beauty of the Sierra Nevada shine through. Whether you choose romantic garden roses, rustic wildflower arrangements, or sophisticated orchids, your flowers should feel like they belong.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>General Tips for Matching Florals to Lake Tahoe Venues</strong></p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><em>Consider Your Venue's Natural Surroundings</em></p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Lake Tahoe venues come with spectacular natural backdrops, towering pines, azure waters, granite peaks, mountain meadows, or sandy beaches. Your florals should enhance, not compete with, these features. Lakeside venues benefit from florals that complement the water (blues, purples, whites,  soft colors), while mountain-forest settings pair beautifully with warmer tones and natural elements. And to tie in additional colors, think Lake Tahoe sunset colors. </p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><em>Match Formality Levels</em></p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A rustic barn venue calls for different florals than a luxury resort. Ensure your floral style matches your venue's level of formality. Upscale venues like Edgewood Tahoe or The Ritz-Carlton can handle more elaborate, luxurious florals, while casual venues like Olympic Valley Stables shine with loose, organic designs.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><em>Work With Your Venue's Color Scheme</em></p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Many Lake Tahoe venues feature natural wood, stone, or specific interior colors. Visit your venue and note these permanent elements. Choose floral colors that complement rather than clash with the venue's existing palette. Rich wood tones pair beautifully with warm florals (peach, coral, burgundy), while lighter spaces can handle bolder colors.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><em>Plan for Tahoe's Climate</em></p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Lake Tahoe's high elevation, intense sun, and temperature fluctuations affect flowers differently than sea-level venues. Choose hardy blooms that can withstand these conditions. Your florist should know which flowers travel well to altitude, how to keep arrangements hydrated, and which varieties hold up in outdoor settings. On the first day of summer 2025, we got SNOW(!), and then the sun came out later in the day for the ceremony. </p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><em>Consider Seasonal Availability and Timing</em></p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Some Lake Tahoe venues are seasonal (only available during certain months), which naturally influences your floral options. Winter venues pair perfectly with evergreens and winter blooms, while summer-only locations can showcase the full range of seasonal flowers. Always choose flowers that are in season during your wedding month for the best quality and value.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>Edgewood Tahoe Resort</strong></p></div>
