Before founding Golden Florals, my background was rooted in science. The "Grown, Not Flown" Difference</h3><p style="white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">When flowers are flown in from across the globe, they are cut entirely too early, treated with heavy synthetic preservatives, and stripped of their natural vitality. By the time they arrive at your venue, they are running on empty.</p><p style="white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Because we operate our own flower farm plots right here in the Sierra Nevada foothills, your wedding flowers are harvested at the absolute peak of their natural life cycle. They thrive in the bright, cool early-season mountain air.</p></li><li><p style="white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">Late Summer &amp; Early Fall (August – October):&nbsp;This is when the landscape shifts to rich texture.
