<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content><p style="white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">If you're getting married at The Ritz-Carlton Lake Tahoe, you already know the venue does the heavy lifting. Perched mid-mountain at Northstar California in Truckee, it's California's only AAA Five Diamond mountain resort, and it shows. Soaring wood-beam ceilings, floor-to-ceiling windows, wrought-iron chandeliers, and views of the Sierra Nevada that stop guests in their tracks.</p><p style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">So what does that mean for your flowers?</p><p style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">It means your florals need to be just as intentional as the setting, not competing with it, but becoming part of it.</p><p style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true"><strong>Let the landscape lead</strong></p><p style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">The Ritz-Carlton Lake Tahoe is a venue defined by its natural surroundings. The Woods ceremony site sits beneath towering Sierra pines that filter afternoon light in a way no ballroom can replicate. The Fireside Terrace is anchored by a seven-foot fireplace with views stretching beyond the gondola towers. The Meadows offers an open alpine backdrop that changes with every season.</p><p style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">The worst thing you can do in a setting like this is bring in flowers that look like they were shipped from a warehouse. Tight, uniform, tropical blooms fight the mountain landscape instead of honoring it.</p><p style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">What works here is wild, seasonal, and locally rooted, the kind of florals that look like they belong to the Sierra Nevadas because they do.</p><p style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true"><strong>What we recommend for Ritz-Carlton weddings</strong></p><p style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">For ceremonies in The Woods or The Meadows, we love loose, organic arrangements that echo the wildflower meadows of the Sierra Nevada. Think garden roses, ranunculus, sweet peas, anemones, dahlias and foraged greenery, nothing too structured, nothing too perfect.</p><p style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">For the Ritz-Carlton Ballroom or Pines Ballroom receptions, you have the ceiling height and the architecture to go big. Ground-level installations, tall arrangements in statement vessels, and lush tablescapes that nod to the mountain setting while still feeling elevated and luxurious.</p><p style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">For the Fireside Terrace cocktail hour, keep it simple. A few well-placed arrangements that don't compete with the fireplace or the views. This is a space that sells itself, let it.</p><p style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true"><strong>A real Golden Flowers wedding at the Ritz</strong></p><p style="text-align: start; ; white-space:pre-wrap;" data-rte-preserve-empty="true">We had the privilege of designing florals for Sara's wedding at The Ritz-Carlton Truckee, a couple who wanted a luxe look without the full-service price tag. We designed the ceremony installation and bouquets, and adorned the tables with bud vases.
