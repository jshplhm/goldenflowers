Checked against Squarespace's own list

Squarespace's URL Mappings export (the old site's 31 hand-built redirects) was cross-checked line by line on July 9. Every rule is accounted for on the new site. Two deliberate differences:

Squarespace ruleWhat we do instead
Retail shop URLs (order-flowers, send-flowers, sendflowers, the-vday-shop) → grassvalleyflowers.comThey stay on our site and land on the home page, since the wedding studio no longer runs a retail shop. Easy to switch to grassvalleyflowers.com if that business should get the traffic.
/contact → /weddingflowersWe send it to the consultation form, which is what someone typing /contact is actually looking for.

The old /llms.txt mapping is covered too: the new site serves its own up-to-date llms.txt directly.

Kept exactly as they were

These never needed redirects because the new site uses the identical addresses: the home page, /about, /consultation-form, and all 35 blog posts, which kept their full /natures-canvas-lake-tahoe-wedding-flowers/<post> addresses letter for letter. (One exception surfaced July 9: the soft-goth post's old URL ended in "nevadas" while the new one drops the s — that spelling now redirects too, listed above.)

If anyone lands on an address not covered above, they see our branded "wandered off the trail" page with paths back to the portfolio, venues, and consultation form — never a bare error page.
