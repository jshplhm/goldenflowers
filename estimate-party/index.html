Four ways to ask about the wedding party

Every variant below is live and drives the real estimator model, with all the other answers held at their defaults, so the only thing moving the numbers is the wedding party. Tap through them and pick the one that reads clearest. None of them ever shows a price per bouquet or per boutonniere.

Two things worth deciding at the same time

Whether the money moves per person or in bands. Flip the switch at the top and watch variant A. Per person, one more boutonniere moves the total most of the time. In bands, it only moves when the party crosses into a bigger size, which is closer to how a real quote behaves and makes it much harder to read a unit price back out of the tool. Bands also fix something that currently looks broken: with the total rounded to the nearest $250, adding a single boutonniere often changes nothing at all, and there is no way for anyone to tell that is on purpose.

The partner's boutonniere. Today's slider counts people standing with you, which excludes the two of you, so the second boutonniere is priced nowhere. Variant C names it. A, B and D need a line of copy or a quiet plus one in the math.

Whichever wins, two small things change downstream: the handoff recap line in _includes/estimator.html stops saying "carrying or wearing flowers", and copy.handoff.party_none in _data/estimator.yml needs wording that still makes sense.
