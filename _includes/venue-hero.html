{%- comment -%} Venue hero photograph. Params: v (the venue's data file). Live venue pages have no photography at all: thirteen pages of pure text about places whose whole appeal is how they look. This is the slot for it. A venue fills `hero_photo` one of two ways. hero_photo: wedding: lynn-aaron # a wedding in _data/portfolio_meta.yml photo: lynn-aaron-19.jpg # a file in that wedding's folder alt: "..." reuses a photograph we already own and already credit, and takes the photographer from credits.yml so the credit can never drift from the one on the wedding page. Or: hero_photo: src: "/assets/images/venues/edgewood-tahoe.jpg" alt: "..." credit: "Courtesy of Edgewood Tahoe" for anything else. `credit` is REQUIRED on this second form and the photo is dropped without it: eleven of these venues have no Golden Flowers photograph, so most of what lands here will be someone else's work used with permission, and an uncredited one is the failure mode to design against. /terms promises every photograph on the site is credited and used with permission. No hero_photo and the page renders exactly as it did before. That is the common case today, so it has to look deliberate rather than broken. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign hp = include.v.hero_photo -%} {%- if hp -%} {%- assign src = "" -%} {%- assign credit = hp.credit -%} {%- if hp.wedding and hp.wedding != "" and hp.photo and hp.photo != "" -%} {%- assign src = '/assets/images/portfolio/' | append: hp.wedding | append: '/' | append: hp.photo -%} {%- unless credit and credit != "" -%} {%- assign shooter = site.data.credits[hp.wedding].photography -%} {%- if shooter.name and shooter.name != "" and shooter.name != "TBC" -%} {%- assign credit = shooter.name -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endunless -%} {%- elsif hp.src and hp.src != "" and credit and credit != "" -%} {%- assign src = hp.src -%} {%- endif -%} {%- if src != "" -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endif -%}
