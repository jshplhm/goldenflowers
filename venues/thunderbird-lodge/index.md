    <h2>How we approach Thunderbird Lodge florals.</h2>
    <div class="philosophy-grid">
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Honor the architecture</h3>
        <p>Stone, timber, and craftsmanship define this estate. We design florals with the same sense of permanence, structural, material-forward, and built to belong to the place.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Frame the lake</h3>
        <p>The water is the centerpiece. We keep sightlines open with height, transparency, and negative space so arrangements add to the view rather than compete with it.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Palette from the east shore</h3>
        <p>Granite gray, pine green, and the deep blue of the lake. We bring whites, soft neutrals, and moments of depth that feel native to the shoreline.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>California-sourced, always</h3>
        <p>No flowers flown in from overseas. Every arrangement uses blooms grown in California, from our own plots and the growers we've worked with for years.</p>
