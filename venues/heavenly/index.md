<section class="block-narrow prose">
  <p class="lead">Ride the gondola up and the entire Tahoe basin opens beneath you — lake, ridgelines, and sky in every direction. Weddings at Heavenly trade an enclosed room for one of the biggest views in the Sierra.</p>
  <p>The view is the headliner, so the flowers play a supporting role on purpose: clean, intentional, framing the moment without competing with it. Up here, wind and altitude are real — we design and build so nothing wilts, shifts, or blows away between the gondola and the last dance.</p>
  <p>Getting florals to a mountaintop is its own logistics problem. We plan the timing, transport, and install so the only thing you notice is the view.</p>
</section>
