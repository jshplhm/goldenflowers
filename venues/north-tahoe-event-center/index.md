    <span class="label">Design philosophy</span>
    <h2>How we approach North Tahoe Event Center florals.</h2>
    <div class="philosophy-grid">
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Flowers that transform the room</h3>
        <p>In a blank-canvas hall, florals aren't decoration, they're the design. We use installations, statement centerpieces, and a deliberate palette to give the space architecture and warmth it doesn't have on its own.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Light at the lakefront</h3>
        <p>The ceremony's backdrop is the lake itself. Outdoor arrangements stay airy and transparent so they read against the water and hold up to the open-air North Shore wind and sun.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Local logistics, handled</h3>
        <p>Kings Beach is a short drive from our Incline Village base. We do early site visits, install on the timeline the venue allows, and return for a clean same-day strike.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Grown in California, never flown in</h3>
        <p>No floral foam, no flowers flown in from South America or Europe. Every stem is grown in California, from our own plots and the growers we trust.</p>
