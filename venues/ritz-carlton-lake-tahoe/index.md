    <h2>How we approach Ritz-Carlton florals.</h2>
    <div class="philosophy-grid">
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Match the finish</h3>
        <p>The Ritz-Carlton sets a high bar for polish. We design florals that hold that standard, clean, intentional, and finished down to the last stem at every table.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Design for the season</h3>
        <p>A four-season venue means very different weddings. We design around what's at peak, soft and abundant in summer, deep and structural in winter, so the flowers belong to the date.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Bridge inside and out</h3>
        <p>Ceremonies in the pines, receptions in the ballroom. We carry a cohesive palette and language across both, so the day feels like one designed event rather than two.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>California-sourced, always</h3>
        <p>No flowers flown in from overseas. Every arrangement uses blooms grown in California, from our own plots and the growers we've worked with for years.</p>
