<section class="block-narrow prose">
  <p class="lead">Valhalla is a 1920s Grand Hall and boathouse on Lake Tahoe's south shore, set among old-growth pines a few steps from the water. It is one of the most atmospheric venues on the lake — timber, stone, and that filtered Sierra light.</p>
  <p>A room with this much character doesn't want to be covered up. We design florals that work <em>with</em> the wood and the pines: textural, seasonal, a little wild at the edges, never fussy. The Grand Hall takes statement installations beautifully; the boathouse and lawn ceremonies call for something looser and lakeside.</p>
  <p>Because Valhalla is part of a state historic site, load-in and breakdown follow specific rules and timing. We've worked within them before and plan the day so the constraints never reach you.</p>
</section>
