<!-- Design Philosophy for Venue -->
<section class="section">
  <div class="container">
    <span class="label">Design philosophy</span>
    <h2>How we approach Edgewood florals.</h2>
    <div class="philosophy-grid">
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Work with the view, not against it</h3>
        <p>The lakeside terrace has one of the most photographed backdrops in the Tahoe basin. We design installations that add to that context, never competing. Scale, transparency, and negative space are as important as the flowers themselves.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Palette pulled from the landscape</h3>
        <p>Edgewood's palette is the Nevada sage, the granite gray, the lake's deep blue-green. We bring whites, soft sage, and warm ivory that feel native, with moments of depth for drama when couples want it.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Proportions for the space</h3>
        <p>The Edgewood Pavilion is a grand space. Low-profile centerpieces at tables with ceilings this high can disappear. We design installations and centerpieces to read at scale, visible from across the room, beautiful up close.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>California-sourced, always</h3>
        <p>No flowers imported from South America or Europe. Every arrangement at Edgewood uses blooms grown in California, from our own plots, and from the growers we've worked with for years. The environmental footprint matches the venue's own values.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
