        <p>The Hellman-Ehrman Mansion sits on the West Shore at Sugar Pine Point, a historic lakefront estate surrounded by old-growth pine and protected parkland. Ceremonies happen outdoors, on the lawn in front of the mansion and along the water, with the lake and the Sierra crest as the backdrop.</p>

<!-- Design philosophy -->
<section class="section">
  <div class="container">
    <span class="label">Design philosophy</span>
    <h2>How we approach Hellman-Ehrman florals.</h2>
    <div class="philosophy-grid">
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Gathered, not imported</h3>
        <p>The estate is wrapped in pine, fern, and lakeside meadow. We pull palettes and textures from that, soft whites, sage, warm naturals, foraged greenery, so the flowers read as part of the forest, not flown in to it.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Built for open air</h3>
        <p>Outdoor lakefront ceremonies get full sun and afternoon wind off the water. We design installations and bouquets to hold up in that exposure and still look effortless in photos at golden hour.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Foam-free by necessity and by choice</h3>
        <p>A protected state park can't take floral foam or shed material. Everything we build here is foam-free and compostable, mechanically sound enough to travel in, strike cleanly, and leave the lawn exactly as we found it.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>California-sourced, always</h3>
        <p>No flowers imported from South America or Europe. Every stem comes from our own plots or the California growers we've worked with for years, a footprint that matches the parkland you're standing in.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
