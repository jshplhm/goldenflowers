    <span class="label">Design philosophy</span>
    <h2>How we approach Schaffer's Camp florals.</h2>
    <div class="philosophy-grid">
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Built for altitude</h3>
        <p>Thin, dry air is hard on cut flowers. We choose hardy varieties, condition them thoroughly, and build with water sources that keep everything alive through a full day on the ridge.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Glass and fire</h3>
        <p>The lodge is timber, stone, and light. We design warm, textural arrangements for the fireside interior and crisp, structural pieces that hold up against the windows and the view.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Indoors and out</h3>
        <p>Ceremonies on the deck, dinner by the fire. We carry one palette and language across both so the day reads as a single designed event.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Planned around the gondola</h3>
        <p>Every stem rides the gondola to the top. We plan the build, packing, and load-in around that, so setup is calm and the flowers arrive at full freshness.</p>
      </div>
