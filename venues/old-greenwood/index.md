<section class="block-narrow prose">
  <p class="lead">Old Greenwood sits in the Martis Valley just outside Truckee — a Jack Nicklaus course framed by open meadow, pine forest, and the Sierra crest beyond. The light is high and clear, and the setting is wide and golden in late summer.</p>
  <p>A venue this expansive wants florals with presence. We design with scale and movement so arrangements read across a big lawn and a big sky — grasses, branching stems, and seasonal blooms that hold up to mountain sun and wind through a full day.</p>
  <p>It's an easy venue to under-design. We make sure the flowers carry the open space rather than getting lost in it.</p>
</section>
