    <span class="label">Design philosophy</span>
    <h2>How we approach High Camp florals.</h2>
    <div class="philosophy-grid">
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Built for altitude</h3>
        <p>Thin, dry air is hard on cut flowers. We choose hardy varieties, condition them thoroughly, and build with water sources that keep everything alive through a full day above the valley.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Scaled for the view</h3>
        <p>The backdrop is granite and sky for miles. Small, low arrangements disappear here. We design installations and centerpieces with the height, transparency, and structure to hold the space.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Palette from the alpine</h3>
        <p>The High Camp palette is granite gray, dry-grass gold, and the deep green of the tree line. We lean into wild, textural, of-the-mountain designs, with drama on request.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="philosophy-item">
        <h3>Planned around the tram</h3>
        <p>Every stem rides the aerial tram to the top. We plan the build, packing, and load-in around that, so setup is calm and the flowers arrive at full freshness.</p>
